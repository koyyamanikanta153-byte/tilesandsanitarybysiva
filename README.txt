TILE & SANITARYWARE SHOWROOM WEBSITE
===================================

This is a responsive, SEO-ready static website template.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Replace "Your Showroom" with your actual showroom/brand name.
2. Replace phone, email, address and city.
3. Replace https://www.yourshowroom.com with your real domain.
4. Add your actual logo.
5. Add real showroom/product/project images inside assets/images/.
   Recommended filenames: hero.jpg, tiles.jpg, sanitary.jpg, bathroom.jpg, brands.jpg.
6. Replace placeholder brand names with brands you are authorised to sell.
7. Connect the contact form to WhatsApp, Formspree, your CRM or backend.
8. Create individual brand/product/location pages as needed.
9. Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
10. Add Google Business Profile link and LocalBusiness schema with exact NAP details.
